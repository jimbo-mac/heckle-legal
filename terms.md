# The Heckle Uploader: Terms of Service

_Last updated: November 11, 2026_

The Heckle Uploader ("the app") is a private tool run by the owner of The Heckle to upload short video clips to The Heckle's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign up for it or use it.

## Use of the app
- The app only uploads videos to the TikTok account that signed in to it, and only as drafts. The account owner reviews each draft and decides whether to post it in the TikTok app.
- Videos uploaded through the app are clips of creators who have given The Heckle permission to share their content. Each post credits the original creator and links to the original video.
- Use of TikTok through the app is also subject to [TikTok's Terms of Service](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/terms-of-service) and [Community Guidelines](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines).

## Content removal
If you own content shared by The Heckle and want it removed, open an issue on this repository and it will be taken down promptly.
