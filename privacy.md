# The Heckle Uploader: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 11, 2026_

The Heckle Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of The Heckle to upload video drafts to The Heckle's own TikTok account.

## What the app accesses
When the account owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives:
- Basic profile information (display name and account ID), used only to confirm which account is connected.
- Permission to upload videos as drafts to that account's TikTok inbox.

## How it's stored
TikTok sign-in tokens are stored only on the account owner's own computer. They are never sold, shared, or sent anywhere except back to TikTok to upload videos.

## What the app doesn't do
- It doesn't collect information from TikTok viewers or any other users.
- It doesn't use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers.
- It doesn't sell or share any data with third parties.

## Deleting data
The account owner can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok's settings (Security → Manage app permissions) and delete the stored token from their computer.

## Changes
This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
